What is DailyMate Lite?

DailyMate Lite is a phrase-practice app by Lumi Studio with 8,400 original cue-and-reply pairs in 50 learning languages, including English, Japanese and Korean. The pairs are organised into 14 topics and 84 real-life situations, such as airport security, hotel check-in, asking directions and ordering food, across Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced levels. You can hear every phrase with on-device system voices at adjustable speed.

Who is it for?

  • Travellers who want to rehearse what to say before a trip.
  • People who want to learn a whole exchange rather than isolated words.
  • People learning more than one language, since each language keeps its own progress and saved phrases.
  • People who prefer not to create an account.

What is free and how is it paid for?

You can choose any learning language and practise the complete Travel topic at Beginner level for free. Other topics and levels stay visible through genuine previews. One in-app purchase unlocks every topic, all three levels, the complete 8,400-pair library, unlimited saved phrases, interactive widgets, Apple Watch and App Intents. There is no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Is DailyMate Lite free?

The Travel topic at Beginner level is free in every learning language; one purchase unlocks the rest.

Is there a subscription?

No. It is a one-time unlock.

Do I need an account?

No. DailyMate Lite does not need an account.

Is it a translation app?

No. It is for practising ready-made phrases and their replies.
